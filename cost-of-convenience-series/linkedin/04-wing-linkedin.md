# LinkedIn Promo — Part Four: Dear Adam, What Happens When There's No Reason Left to Leave?

## Image prompt (for Midjourney / DALL-E / Ideogram / Firefly)

Editorial photograph, cinematic, late-afternoon light. A suburban
backyard, seen from a slightly elevated angle. A small delivery drone
hovers a few feet above the lawn, just having released a package tied
by a thin line — the package sits on the grass, the drone's downward
light and rotor-blur visible above it. Its shadow stretches long across
the yard in the low sun. The back door of the house is closed. Near the
fence, a dog stands mid-bark, alert, facing the drone — the only visible
sign of life in the frame. No people, no faces, no logos, no readable
text on the drone or package. Warm gold light on the grass, the sky
behind the drone cooling to pale blue-grey. Shot on a 35mm lens,
slightly wide, subtle film grain, in the style of a Bloomberg
Businessweek or The Economist cover photograph. Horizontal composition,
negative space in the sky for a headline overlay. Mood: ordinary and
faintly eerie — a transaction completing itself with nobody home to
notice.

**Spec:** 1600×840px (~1.91:1). Midjourney: `--ar 1.91:1 --style raw`.

**Backup prompt** (if the drone-plus-dog composition feels too busy):
drop the dog, keep the drone and its shadow on an otherwise empty lawn,
with one detail added — a porch light left on despite it being daytime,
implying someone is home but simply isn't coming outside.

Lead with **A** — the drone-in-the-backyard image is the strongest
concrete scene in the whole series and does the hook's job better than
any argument could open cold. Save **B**'s stat ("three times a week,
more than they call their mother") for the first comment or a
follow-up post if engagement is strong; it's a great second beat.

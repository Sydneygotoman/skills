How it works
Upload a supplier price list (CSV or TXT) or paste invoice text. The parser reads line items, matches ingredient names to the price book, and proposes price updates. You approve each change before anything updates.
Supported formats
📊 CSV — exported supplier price lists (item,price)
📝 TXT or pasted text — one item per line with a price
📄 PDF / photos — copy the text out and paste it below; the browser can't read them directly
